Luxy Documentation

A bonding-curve launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Deploy tokens, let the curve price them, and graduate into public liquidity — all on-chain, all deterministic.

1. Introduction

Luxy is a bonding-curve launchpad deployed on Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663). It lets anyone deploy a token in a single transaction, priced by a deterministic curve that reads on-chain state only.

There is no presale, no team allocation, and no special-access round. Every token follows the same four-state lifecycle: Deploy → Discover → Graduate → Earn.

Key properties

  • One-signature deploy. Token + curve contract deployed atomically.
  • On-chain quotes. The swap screen reads the same function that executes your trade.
  • Automatic graduation. At the reserve target, remaining supply + ETH are paired and seeded into a public AMM.
  • Non-custodial. No funds ever sit with Luxy — trades settle directly against the curve or pool.

2. Bonding Curve

The curve prices every buy and sell from on-chain reserves. Buys lift supply and push the quote up; sells return tokens and walk it back down.

P(s) = P₀ + ΔP · s²
P(s) — quote at supply s
P₀ — starting price (floor)
ΔP — curve steepness
s — tokens sold so far

Why convex?

A quadratic curve (s²) means early buyers get the best price, and the quote rises faster as supply approaches the target. Late entrants pay more — front-running buys you a worse price, not a better one.

Quote execution

Every quote — on the Swap page and inside each token's trade panel — reads the same getQuote() function. The quote you see is the exact calculation your trade will execute — not a separate off-chain estimate.

3. Launchpad

Deploying a token requires three things: a name, a ticker, and an optional description. That's it — no code, no reserved allocation, no whitelist.

Deployment flow

  • User fills in name, ticker, and optional metadata (image, X link, Telegram, website).
  • One transaction deploys the token contract and its associated bonding-curve pricing contract atomically.
  • The token appears on the Launchpad immediately — listed but not yet tradeable until the first buy hits the curve.

Metadata

Metadata (name, ticker, image, social links) is stored off-chain via the Luxy registry. The launchpad page displays it; the contract enforces the financial parameters.

4. Trading Limits

Every Luxy token ships with built-in trading guardrails, enforced at the contract level — not just in the interface.

Max wallet enforcement

The 5% wallet cap is checked on every transfer at the token contract level. A buy that would push a wallet over 5% reverts. Curve and pool addresses are exempt.

5. Graduation

When the curve's reserve target is met, the token graduates into a public AMM. This is automatic — nobody triggers it, nobody can stop it.

Graduation mechanics

  • At ≈3.96 ETH reserve, the curve stops quoting — it will no longer accept buy/sell transactions.
  • Remaining token supply + collected ETH are paired at the migration ratio and deposited as opening liquidity in a Uniswap V3-style pool.
  • LP position is locked — tokens are never returned to the creator or burned.
  • Trading continues on the AMM. The curve at this point is done.

What happens to unsold tokens?

Unsold tokens are paired with collected ETH and deposited as LP. Nothing sits idle — everything goes into the pool.

6. Trading

Trades are sized in exact-token or exact-ETH terms. The curve settles atomically — your signature includes a slippage floor, and a worse fill reverts.

Slippage presets

  • 0.5% · 1% · 3% presets for quick selection
  • Custom slippage supported
  • Minimum-received value is enforced on-chain

Post-graduation

After graduation, trading moves to the AMM pool with standard swap mechanics. Slippage protection works the same way — your signed minimum-received guards against sandwiching.

7. Referrals

Any wallet can register a referral code. Earn a share of protocol fees from every trade a referred wallet makes.

  • One code per referrer. Permanent once set.
  • Tiered share. Your cut scales up as referred volume grows.
  • Withdraw anytime. No lock, no minimum.
  • Referral accounting is on-chain and queryable via events.

8. Security

Luxy is non-custodial at execution, not governance-free. The boundary between code guarantees and administrative controls is documented below.

Contract guarantees

  • Trade pricing. Priced by the curve contract, settled atomically.
  • Slippage protection. Signed minimum-received is checked on-chain.
  • Max wallet. The 5% cap is enforced on every transfer.
  • Pool protection. The destination pool exists before the token is public and stays locked until the curve completes.

Governance scope

  • Launch access can be paused or gated behind approval.
  • Specific addresses can be blocked from trading (platform tool, not creator control).
  • Fee parameters for new launches are set by governance, within hard-coded contract limits.

Agent key isolation (if applicable)

Any off-chain agent used for recommendations holds a separate signer that cannot move tokens — it can only publish opinions on-chain.

9. FAQ

Why didn't my quote exactly match my fill?

The quote reflects curve state at the moment you asked for it. Any trade that lands before yours moves the price — that's what your slippage tolerance bounds.

Why can't I buy more of a token?

You've likely hit the per-transaction cap (5% of target) or the max-wallet limit (5% of supply). Split buys across multiple transactions.

What happens to unsold curve tokens at graduation?

Remaining supply + collected ETH are paired and deposited as opening liquidity. Nothing is returned or burned.

Does staking pay a fixed rate?

No. Displayed APR is a snapshot that moves with emissions, LP price, and participation. Treat it as an estimate.

What chain is Luxy on?

Luxy is deployed exclusively on Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663).
